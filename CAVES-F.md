#### F. Hobgoblin Lair:

* Entrance guarded by a stout, barred door. On the door is a warning: “Come in. We’d like to have you for dinner!”
* Skulls line the walls.
* If inspecting the door, a roll of 6 will reveal a secret mechanism allowing a person outside to slide the bar back so the portal can be entered.
* If attempting to force door open, must make a strength contest roll of 4+, but the noise will alert area 26.
23. Common Room:
    * 12 hobgoblins.
    * The hobgoblins are watching the east door which communicates with the goblin lair. They are battle-ready.
24. Torture Chamber/Playroom/Food Storage:
    * 2 Hobgoblin Torturers - Strikes: 3, To-Hit 5.
    * One of the hobgoblins has a whip that, if scoring a hit, does no damage but stuns and knocks the target prone for 1-2 melee rounds. Once closely engaged, the whip is no longer effective and will be discarded for a sword.
    * There are implements of torture and a chest with 150 gp.
    * There are 6 prisoners chained to the wall with a key hanging on the wall in the southwest corner.
    * Prisoner 1 - half-dead human merchant. If rescued and returned to the Keep, the guild will pay 100 gp reward.
    * Prisoner 2 - orc who will fight goblin and hobgoblins gladly, but will seek escape from players at his firs chance.
    * Prisoner 3 - a man-at-arms who will take service with the players for 1 year if the offer is mad
    * Prisoner 4 - the wife of prisoner 1.
    * Prisoner 5 - a crazed gnoll who will try and get a weapon and attack hisrescuers if freed.
    * Prisoner 6 - a man-at-arms like prisoner 3.
25. Common Chamber:
    * Meal and meeting place of the tribe.
    * 9 adult hobgoblins and 9 young (who will not fight)
26. Guard Room:
    * 6 hobgoblins.
27. Armory:
    * 3 hobgoblins.
    * Several melee and ranged weapons, shields, and armor.
    * If searching carefully, there is Dragon Scale Armor, and a Helmet of Champions. The hobgoblins have not recognized the magical armor.
28. Storeroom:
    * 1 hobgoblin.
    * Stolen goods from goblins kept here.
29. Guard Room:
    * 4 hobgoblins.
    * Two will alert ares 30 and 31, then return to fight
30. Hobgoblin Chief’s Quarters:
    * Hobgoblin Chief - Strikes: 3, To-Hit: 5.
    * 4 hobgoblin guards.
    * Iron chest has nothing of value, but there is a hidden compartment with 800 gp and a potion of poison (see Magic Items below).
31. Guard Room:
    * 4 hobgoblins.
    * They are alert to danger and will pass word to areas 29, 30, and 27 as required.
