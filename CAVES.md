### The Caves of Chaos

#### A. Kobold Lair

* There are 8 kobolds hiding in the trees near the entrance that will attempt to ambush players.
* Just inside the entrance, there is a pit trap (lead players fall into pit with a roll of 4+ unless they are carefully probing ahead; if careful, lead players fall in with a roll of 6). 
* Falling into the pit causes 1 strike of damage and a lid closes on the pit trapping the players.
* The noise attracts creatures from areas 1 and 2.
1. Guard Room:
    * 6 kobold guards present.
    * A kobold will run to warn areas 4 and 6.
2. Giant Rats:
    * There are 10 giant rats.
    * There are gems among the refuse worth 50 gp.
3. Food Storage Room:
    * Door is locked.
    * Lots of food stores, but nothing else.
4. Guard Room:
    * There are 4 kobolds.
    * There are 10 gp in a purse on one of the kobolds.
5. Kobold Chieftain Room:
    * There is 1 kobold chieftain (with key to area 3) and 5 regular kobolds.
    * There is 50 gp hidden in the room.

#### B. Orc Lair

* Inside the entrance, the wall is adorned with heads and skulls.
* There is a hidden window in the wall with the skulls allowing an orc guard to watch the room.
* There are sounds coming from the west.
7. Guard Room:
    * 4 orcs present that, when alerted, will rush to engage.
8. Watch Room:
    * The orc watching the entry will alert guards from area 7, and then rush to flank from the other directio
9. Banquet Area:
    * Larges tables and a great fireplace ar present.
10. Common Room:
    * There are 20 adult orcs present and 9 juveniles (who do not fight)
11. Storage Chamber:
    * Door is locked.
    * There are 3 shields, 17 spears, 2 battle axes, and a crossbow.
12. Orc Leader’s Room:
    * Orc leader - Strikes: 3, To-Hit: 4, Boon to melee.
    * 2 orcs also present.
    * If in danger, leader will get behind a tapestry and open secret door and ask rival tribe for help.
    * There are gems and treasure worth 1000gp in Area t. There is also a hidden niche (roll 4+ to find) with a healing potion an a scroll of Frakvat’s Fire Elemental’s Call.

#### C. Orc Lair:

    * A series of trip wires are used to warn of intruders. If wires are tripped, a heavy weighted net falls and entraps the players for 1-3 turns. Need roll of 6 to notice if searching for traps. Meanwhile, the orcs from area 14 will be there in 1 round.
13. Forgotten Room:
    * Only the 2 orc leaders know about this secret room. They meet here occasionally to discuss ventures and problems.
    * There are several weapons and shields in this room.
14. Common Room:
    * There are 17 adult orcs with 3 juveniles (who do not fight)
15. Common Hall:
    * Meeting and eating room.
    * 10 orcs are here.
16. Leader’s Room:
    * An orc guard is always posted just inside the door and shouts an alarm if anyone attempts entry.
    * Orc leader - Strikes: 3, To-Hit: 4, Boon to melee.
    * There is a sack with 400 gp that is tied shut with a rope of climbing (see Magic Items).

#### D. Goblin Lair:

* Natural cave quickly turns into worked stone tunnels.
* Area is very busy and for every 10 feet traveled, roll 1D6. On a roll of 1, the party will encounter 1D6+2 goblins.
17. Guard Chamber:
    * 6 goblin guards.
    * There is a barrel with 60 spears, a small table, and a keg of water.
18. Guard Chamber:
    * 6 goblin guards.
    * If there is a cry of “BREE-YARK”, 2 guards will rush to the secret door and toss a sack with 250 gp to the ogre (area 22). The ogre will move to attack players.
19. Common Room:
    * 24 adult goblins and 6 young (who do not fight)
    * Food is prepared here.
    * Heaps of bedding, tables, stools, benches make room very cluttered.
    * There is a total of 150 gp among all the goblins.
20. Chieftain’s Room:
    * Goblin Leader - Strikes: 2, To-Hit: 4, Boon to strength
    * 5 goblin guards.
    * Tapestry inlaid with gold and silver strands worth 900 gp (but will burden player carrying it).
    * Locked chest has 300 gp.
21. Storage Chamber:
    * At position g, there are 4 goblin guards.
    * Many barrels, crates, boxes, and sacks.
    * There are containers of food, ale, and wine.
    * Goblins constantly bring in loot from raids.
    * There is a secret door to Area F that hobgoblins use to steal the best foodstuff
    * If the players stay in the room for more than 1 turn, roll 1D6. On a roll of 3+, 4 hobgoblins will enter through the secret door.

#### E. Ogre Cave (Area 22):

* Strong, sour oder present.
* There appears to be a huge bear sprawled asleep in the southwestern area of cave. This is actually a bear skinused for bedding.
* Ogre sits on top of great leather bag in the eastern part of the cave.
* If offer more gold than from the goblins the ogre will take it and stay in his cave and will not fight against the players on 1D6 roll of 2+.
* In the large leather bag are coins and gems worth 1000 gp.
* Hidden under bone heaps in the southern part of the cave are a Death Seeker Bow, a Vindella’s Vale of the Unseen scroll, a healing potion, and a Varnesh’s Grip scroll (see Magic Items below).

#### F. Hobgoblin Lair:

* Entrance guarded by a stout, barred door. On the door is a warning: “Come in. We’d like to have you for dinner!”
* Skulls line the walls.
* If inspecting the door, a roll of 6 will reveal a secret mechanism allowing a person outside to slide the bar back so the portal can be entered.
* If attempting to force door open, must make a strength contest roll of 4+, but the noise will alert area 26.
23. Common Room:
    * 12 hobgoblins.
    * The hobgoblins are watching the east door which communicates with the goblin lair. They are battle-ready.
24. Torture Chamber/Playroom/Food Storage:
    * 2 Hobgoblin Torturers - Strikes: 3, To-Hit 5.
    * One of the hobgoblins has a whip that, if scoring a hit, does no damage but stuns and knocks the target prone for 1-2 melee rounds. Once closely engaged, the whip is no longer effective and will be discarded for a sword.
    * There are implements of torture and a chest with 150 gp.
    * There are 6 prisoners chained to the wall with a key hanging on the wall in the southwest corner.
    * Prisoner 1 - half-dead human merchant. If rescued and returned to the Keep, the guild will pay 100 gp reward.
    * Prisoner 2 - orc who will fight goblin and hobgoblins gladly, but will seek escape from players at his firs chance.
    * Prisoner 3 - a man-at-arms who will take service with the players for 1 year if the offer is mad
    * Prisoner 4 - the wife of prisoner 1.
    * Prisoner 5 - a crazed gnoll who will try and get a weapon and attack hisrescuers if freed.
    * Prisoner 6 - a man-at-arms like prisoner 3.
25. Common Chamber:
    * Meal and meeting place of the tribe.
    * 9 adult hobgoblins and 9 young (who will not fight)
26. Guard Room:
    * 6 hobgoblins.
27. Armory:
    * 3 hobgoblins.
    * Several melee and ranged weapons, shields, and armor.
    * If searching carefully, there is Dragon Scale Armor, and a Helmet of Champions. The hobgoblins have not recognized the magical armor.
28. Storeroom:
    * 1 hobgoblin.
    * Stolen goods from goblins kept here.
29. Guard Room:
    * 4 hobgoblins.
    * Two will alert ares 30 and 31, then return to fight
30. Hobgoblin Chief’s Quarters:
    * Hobgoblin Chief - Strikes: 3, To-Hit: 5.
    * 4 hobgoblin guards.
    * Iron chest has nothing of value, but there is a hidden compartment with 800 gp and a potion of poison (see Magic Items below).
31. Guard Room:
    * 4 hobgoblins.
    * They are alert to danger and will pass word to areas 29, 30, and 27 as required.

#### G. Shunned Cavern:

* Even normal inhabitant of this area, including the ogre, stay away.
* A horrible stench is noticed as soon as players enter the cave area.
32. Empty Gallery:
    * The odor is awful.
    * Bones and rotting corpses present.
    * If players search the corpses, roll 1D6: 
        * 1: owlbear from area 34 arrives; 
        * 2: 2D6 giant rats; 
        * 3: gray ooze from area 34; 
        * 4-6: nothing happens
33. Shallow Pool:
    * This part of cavern is very wet and damp.

    * 3 gray ooze
34. Owlbear’s Den:
    * The owlbear sleeps in the southern part of the den.
    * If searching the corpses in the room, on a roll of 3+, a Tellex’s Turning (see Magic Items below) scroll can be found.

#### H. Bugbear Lair:

* Signs in multiple languages are posted outside the entrance reading “Safety, security, and repose for all humanoids who enter — Welcome! (Come in and report to the first guard on the left for hot meal and bed assignment).”
35. Guard Room:
    * 3 bugbears.
    * Meet is cooking on a brazier.
    * When adventurers enter, the bugbears will offer meat (they do not speak Common), then use the skewers as weapons and strike the first blow.
    * If battle is going poorly for bugbears, they will strike a gong to warn others.
36. Chieftain’s Room:
    * Bugbear Chief - Strikes: 3, To-Hit: 3; crits on a 5+. Key to area 37 is on his belt.
    * 1 other bugbear present.
    * There is a chest on a ledge near the ceiling that can be spotted if the room is carefully searched. It contains 900 gp and 2 potions of healing.
37. Spoils Room:
    * Heavy door is locked (key on the chieftain from area 36).
    * There are food stuffs, casks of ale an wine, dried herbs, and a Shield of Surtur.
38. Common Room:
    * 10 adult bugbears and 3 young (will not fight)
39. Guard Room:
    * 5 bugbears.
    * On the wall opposite of the stairs, there are keys to areas 40 and 41.
40. Slave Pen:
    * The iron door is secured by a bar, chain, and heavy padlock.
    * Inside are the following slaves: 3 kobolds, 1 goblin, 4 orcs, 2 humans, 1 dwarf, and 1 elf.
    * All slaves will fight if given weapon
41. Slave Pen:
    * The iron door is secured by a bar, chain, and heavy padlock.
    * Inside are the following slaves: 3 hobgoblins, 2 gnolls, 1 (rebel) bugbear, and 1 huge human,
    * The human is a fighter: Strikes 4, To-Hit 4. He is prone to fits of berserk fury an will occasionally strike a friend instead ofa foe (50% chance).

#### K. Shrine of Evil Chaos:
* A foul draft issues from the entrance.
* Oddly bloated trees surround the cave.
* There is the palpable feeling of evil.
* If the players do not enter with stealth (stealth roll needed is 4+), 8 zombies will lumber in.
51. Boulder-Filled Passage:
    * It is clear that large rocks and boulders have been placed here to seal off the tunnel. It will take significant time to clear the path.
    * The path leads to the Cave of the Unknown.
52. Hall of Skeletons:
    * An audience chamber with a dias and throne-like chair. The chair is inset with 4 large red gems (500 gp each).
    * There are 12 skeletons propped up against the wall and are not animated.
    * If the player touch the dias or the throne, the skeletons come to life and attack. The skeletons each have an amulet that protects them from turning.
53. Guard Room:
    * 8 zombies protected from turning by an amulet.
    * Anyone entering will be attacked unless wearing temple garb and have an amulet identical to the one the undead guards and priests wear.
54. Acolyte’s Chamber:
    * 4 Acolytes - strikes: 1, to-hit: 3; act as conjurers with a spell circle of Necromancer; magic defense 2D6.
    * There are various books and scrolls of an evil nature.
    * The acolytes wear red robes with black cowls and have a distinctive amulet.
55. Chapel of Evil Chaos:
    * The chapel is made of red stone walls with a floor of mosaic checkerboard of black and red.
    * There is a huge tapestry on the south wall.
    * Four pillars support the domed ceiling.
    * Stone alter stained with dried blood, and atop there are 4 bronze cups, a shallow bowl, an ornate pitcher, and a ceremonial knife. The pieces are worth 3000 gp.
    * Touching any of the cursed alter items, a player must roll 3+ on a magic contest (success provides them with a feeling that the object is of great evil), or become unable to put the item down, and after 6 days, they will become a servant of evil returning to the Shrine.
    * Some form of dispel magic or bless may save those who are cursed.
56. Adept’s Chamber:
    * 4 Adepts - Strikes: 2, To-Hit: 4.
    * One will cast a fear spell against the party (power level will depend on the magic save rolls).
    * One will cast a drain life spell.
    * One will cast a fireball spel
    * One will cast a magic missile spell.
    * If hard pressed, the adepts will attempt to flee and warn their master by strikin the great bell in area 58
    * There are several books of unspeakable evil. If the party contains a Friar and destroys the books, all party members will be restored to full strikes.
57. Hall of Undead Warriors:
    * There are 2 files of undead: 20 skeleton and 20 zombies.
    * Upon striking the bell in area 58, they will issue forth into the temple (area 58).
    * If players enter the hall, the undead will attack unless proper garments and the amulet are worn.
58. Temple of Evil Chaos:
    * Huge arched ceiling. Floor is polished black stone. Walls adorned with tapestries.
    * A great bell of black iron stands near entry.
    * Several benches and pews.
    * There is a dias of black stone with 4 lesser chairs made of bones and one great throne.
    * Upon entry, black candles in 8 large candelabras light.
    * Shapeless forms of purple, yellow, and green will dance and sway on the western wall. 
        * Any player looking at them must make a saving roll of 3+ or begin chanting a hymn to chaotic evil. 
        * Should 3 or more voices be raised, the bell will ring automatically. 
        * A single voice will still alert the Head Chaos Cleric. 
        * Zombie guards will enter in 3 rounds after entry regardless.
59. The Chambers of the Evil Priest:
    * Lavish furnishings.
    * 3 zombies guard room.
    * Head Chaos Cleric - Strikes: 6, To-Hit: 4; 3D6 magic resistance; 2 attacks; uses a Snake Staff.
        * Snake Staff that when hitting target coils around them grappling them (roll 5+ to escape); cleric can recall staff a command.
        * Can cast drain life, hold person, and cause fear but must touch the target to cast
        * He has a potion that will turn him into a gaseous form if he needs to escape.
    * There is 1000 gp in treasure in the room.
60. Guest Chamber:
    * Lower room for important guests.
61. Torture Chamber:
    * Various implements of torture here.
    * Torturer - Strikes: 4, To-Hit: 4; 2 attacks (axe), Boon to melee and strength.
62. The Crypt:
    * Door is bolted shut.
    * Several coffins and large sarcopha
    * The 6th tomb opened will contain a wight.
    * Inside a secret compartment in the wight’s tomb, there are 
        * Doomforged Weapon, 
        * Friar Ulthrid’s Holy Light scroll (see Magic Items below)
        * Silvered dagger worth 800 gp.
63. Storage Chamber:
    * Piles of boxes, crates, barrels, and sacks.
    * If the players spend time searching the room, a gelatinous cube will move down the corridor and block it.
64. Cell:
    * The door is locked and barred. A window is set in the door.
    * Several skeletons are chained to the wall.
    * From a distance, there appears to be a maiden chained to the wall, but this is actually a medusa. The medusa will try and turn some of the party to stone, then offer to use a potion of stone to flesh revive them if only she were to be released (which she does indeed have). If freed, however, she will try and “stone” her rescuers.
