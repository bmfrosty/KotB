#### E. Ogre Cave (Area 22):

* Strong, sour odor present.
* There appears to be a huge bear sprawled asleep in the southwestern area of cave. This is actually a bear skinused for bedding.
* Ogre sits on top of great leather bag in the eastern part of the cave.
* If offer more gold than from the goblins the ogre will take it and stay in his cave and will not fight against the players on 1D6 roll of 2+.
* In the large leather bag are coins and gems worth 1000 gp.
* Hidden under bone heaps in the southern part of the cave are a Death Seeker Bow, a Vindella’s Vale of the Unseen scroll, a healing potion, and a Varnesh’s Grip scroll (see Magic Items below).
